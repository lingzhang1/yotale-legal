YoTale Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Who we are. YoTale is published by Ling Zhang ("YoTale", "we", "us"), a sole proprietor in California, USA. Contact: privacy@yotale.ai.

YoTale turns the photos and videos you capture into short edited videos. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do.

1. Short version

  • Your photos and videos start on your iPhone and stay there unless you use an AI feature or turn on backup.
  • To make a video, we send sampled frames (still images) from your clips to AI providers for analysis and editing. We upload your full original photos and videos only if you turn on Auto Backup.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics software.
  • Apple handles your payment. We never see your card details or Apple ID password.
  • You can ask us to delete your data by emailing privacy@yotale.ai.

2. What we collect

Account. Your email address (used to sign in with a one-time code through Amazon Cognito) and a random account identifier we create.

Content you create or import.

  • On your device: your Moments (photos, videos, audio), Stories, your instructions and the videos we help you make live in the app's storage on your iPhone.
  • Sent to our servers for AI features: frames sampled from your Moments, small contact-sheet images, your written instructions and Story details, and technical timing information. We use them to understand your footage, plan the edit, suggest ideas and recommend music.
  • Saved by us so the service works: the results of the AI analysis (descriptions and structured notes about each Moment) and the plan that describes how your video is cut. We keep these so we do not have to analyze the same footage again.
  • Optional cloud backup: if you turn on Auto Backup, your original Moments and the videos you generate are uploaded to private cloud storage tied to your account. It is off by default.

Location (optional). If you allow location access, we can store where a Moment was captured. You can turn it off in iOS Settings. It is saved with the Moment and with the copy saved to your Photos library.

Purchases. If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We receive a signed transaction from Apple that includes a transaction identifier, the product, dates, renewal or expiry status and refund status. We do not receive your payment card details. We create a random "app account" identifier so Apple can link a purchase to your YoTale account.

Usage and diagnostics. When you use AI features we keep technical records tied to your account identifier, such as which operation ran, when, how long it took, the size of the request, error codes, the AI model used and its cost. We use these to run the service, enforce usage limits, prevent abuse and fix problems. We do not put your photos, videos, instructions or email address in these records.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the features you ask for: analyze footage, plan and render videos, suggest ideas and music, keep your Stories in sync across your devices, and back up if you enable it.
  • To run subscriptions: confirm your purchase with Apple, give you the allowances you paid for, and handle renewals, refunds and restores.
  • To keep the service safe and reliable: limit usage, detect abuse, measure costs, debug and improve quality.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not train our own AI models on your photos or videos.

4. Who receives your data

We use service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, storage, databases and sign-in. Our servers run in the United States.
  • AI providers (OpenAI and Google): they receive the frames, images and text described above so they can analyze your footage and plan edits. They process that data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Music provider: receives music search terms and attributes, not your photos or videos.
  • Apple: payments and subscription management.

We may share information if the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or in a sale or reorganization of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to your data unless we tell you otherwise. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use YoTale from another country, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Account and analysis data in our cloud: until you ask us to delete it or delete your account. We keep AI analysis results so footage is not re-analyzed.
  • Backups of your Moments and videos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Operational logs: up to 90 days, unless needed longer to investigate abuse or errors.
  • Payment records: Apple holds the purchase record. If we must keep a minimal record of a payment for tax or accounting, we keep only what the law requires, for as long as the law requires.

Deleting a Moment or video inside the app removes it from your device. It does not by itself remove copies already in your cloud backup or your analysis results. To remove those, contact us as described in section 7.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion: email privacy@yotale.ai from the address on your account and we will help you access, correct or delete your data. We respond within 30 days.
  • Turn features off: Auto Backup and location can be turned off. Without AI features YoTale cannot analyze footage or build videos.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings. Cancelling does not delete your existing videos.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom) you may have additional rights, such as to know, access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a regulator. We honor these rights. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

8. Age

YoTale is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, per-account access controls and least-privilege cloud permissions. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. For material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact
